# ID Photo Maker

**Free passport, visa & ID photo maker that runs entirely in your browser.**

🔗 **Live app:** https://rmrkweb.github.io/id-photo-maker/

Make photos that meet official size and background requirements, then print them on a ready-to-cut sheet. Your photos are processed on your own device and are never uploaded.

## Features

- **Official size presets** for passports, visas and IDs, including the Philippines, United States, United Kingdom, European Union (Schengen), Canada, Australia, New Zealand, India, Nepal, Singapore, Japan, China and the UAE.
- **Custom sizes** in inches, mm, cm or pixels at 150, 300 or 600 DPI.
- **Studio print sizes**: Cute (2×3 in), Wallet (2.5×3.5 in), 2R (3×4 in), 3R (3.5×5 in), 4R (4×6 in) and 5R (5×7 in).
- **Head and eye alignment guides** (crown, eye and chin lines, head oval) with pan, zoom, rotate and flip.
- **Background removal and replacement**: auto cutout, color eyedropper, manual erase and restore brushes, and standard background colors (white, off-white, light blue, royal blue, red, grays) or transparent PNG.
- **Portrait adjustments**: brightness, contrast, saturation, exposure, warmth and sharpness, with a press-and-hold before/after comparison.
- **Multi-photo sheets**: edit several customer photos one after another and print them together on one sheet, for example 4 different wallet-size photos on a single page.
- **Print sheets** in 4R, 3R, 5R, 6R, A4, US Letter or a custom paper size, with automatic photo packing, adjustable margins and spacing, and cutting guides.
- **Export** as JPEG or PNG, or as a print-ready PDF at exact physical size.
- **Saved projects** stored locally in your browser, so you can come back to them later.
- **Installable and offline-capable** as a Progressive Web App on desktop, Android and iOS.

## How to use

1. Open the [live app](https://rmrkweb.github.io/id-photo-maker/).
2. Upload a photo or take one with your camera.
3. Pick a country and document preset, or set a custom size.
4. Align your face with the guides, then replace the background and adjust lighting if needed.
5. Download the single photo, or build a print sheet and export it as an image or PDF.

To install it as an app, use the **Install App** button in the top bar (on iPhone and iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen).

## Privacy

- Photos are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to any server.
- No accounts, no sign-in and no advertisements.
- Saved projects stay in your browser's local storage, and you can delete them at any time.
- The site uses Google Analytics for basic, anonymous usage statistics such as page views. Your photos and projects are never sent to it.

## Presets disclaimer

Presets follow commonly published requirements, but rules change and individual agencies can differ. Always check the current requirements of the issuing authority before submitting a photo.
